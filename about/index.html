About Applied Identities

Applied Identities is an independent behavioral governance company for AI agents. We give an enterprise’s agents signed identities, measure their conduct against the standard the company writes, and keep the record.

We build no functional agents, by design. A party that profits from an agent’s work cannot neutrally judge its conduct. Every board already applies this discipline to its numbers: the company does not sign off on its own books. We apply the same discipline to agent behavior — which is why we don’t build, host, or monetize the agents we measure.

We run under the same governance we sell. The agents that publish our research and run our commerce are governed agents, and their record is public: every identity in the registry, every transaction on the Ledger.

Applied Identities was founded by two operators on their third company together, with three decades of navigating enterprises through technology transitions and a production stack designed and built in-house.
