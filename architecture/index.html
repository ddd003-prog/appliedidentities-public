Architecture

Governance should not become one more place your data lives.

Applied Identities runs across two planes with a strict separation. Behavioral evaluation happens inside your environment, against your agents' own records, with the option of using your own model keys. Only signed, derived findings cross the wire. What reaches us is a score and a proof, never a transcript.

How it works

Two planes, one strict separation.

The observation layer runs inside your perimeter.

Raw conversations, tool calls, and decisions are read and scored locally. They are never transmitted.

Your keys, your environment.

Signing keys are generated inside your environment on installation and never leave it. The proof is yours to produce, not ours to hold.

A lightweight footprint by design.

The edge agent deploys as a single binary or container, alongside what you already run, with no reconstruction of your stack.

Our observer is itself governed.

Our own observation agent carries its own identity and its own record. Audit it with the same tools you use for your agents. The trust proposition is verification, not faith.

Govern your agents without moving your data.

Tell us about your environment. We will show you how the observation layer fits inside your perimeter.
