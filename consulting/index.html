The consulting engagements

Three engagements, one standard.

01Executive Alignment

For the team that needs a common language before it can make a decision.

A structured briefing for your executive team: what an AI agent workforce actually is, what separates the companies scaling it from the majority stalled at pilots, and what the emerging discipline of behavioral governance covers. Built on the current market record, real deployments, real incidents, real regulatory movement, translated into your industry's terms.

You leave with a shared vocabulary, a map of where your organization stands, and the questions your team should be asking vendors, auditors, and each other.

Format: Half-day working session plus a written readout. Delivered on your calendar, at your site or remote.

02Planning and Budget Sprint

For the team building its 2027 plan right now.

Most companies enter budget season with AI spend scattered across departmental experiments and no structure for the decisions that matter: what to fund as a workforce program rather than a tool purchase, how to weigh building against renting, where governance and measurement belong in the budget, and what evidence the board will expect for the money.

The sprint gives your planning cycle that structure. We work with the executives who own the numbers to produce a budget framework for the AI workforce line: categories, decision criteria, sequencing, and the governance costs most first-year budgets miss.

Format: Two to three weeks, fixed fee, timed to your planning calendar. The deliverable is a framework your finance team puts to work in the current cycle.

03Behavioral Diagnostic

For the team ready to put its agents on the record.

The diagnostic turns how your company actually operates, decides, and speaks into a signed behavioral specification for every agent that represents you. We call the method Ground Truth, because that is what it produces: the authoritative written standard your agents are measured against from that point forward.

You leave with a scored assessment of how your agents behave today, a signed specification set your organization owns, and a readiness read your board can act on. This is the first step of any platform deployment, and it stands on its own whether or not you deploy.

Format: Nine weeks baseline, fixed fee, sized to organizational complexity, with a two-to-three-week pre-engagement for scoping and access.

From pilot to steady state

What comes after the engagements.

The engagements produce the standard. The steps below put agents on the record against it, and keep them there.

04Pilot

For the team that wants to see the system work on its own agents before it commits.

A pilot runs the whole system on a small, real scope: a written standard for the agents in scope, authority issued against it, behavior measured independently, and a record kept. It begins with the diagnostic work above and ends with agents on the record, so the question at the end is not whether the approach works but how far to take it.

You leave with your first agents on a signed standard, the first measurements of how they behave against it, and a record your board and your counterparties can check.

Format: A 90-day run, all in, sized to the agents in scope. Deliberately simple to start.

05Deployment

For the team rolling governance out across the agents that represent the company.

Deployment takes what the pilot proved and stands it up across the fleet: the behavioral standard extended to every agent in scope, authorization issued and scoped per agent, the measurement layer running, and the record public. Our team works alongside yours, or alongside a certified partner, through the stand-up.

Format: Scoped to the fleet, delivered as one combined engagement with the stand-up of the platform.

06Managed Governance

For the team operating a governed agent workforce day to day.

Steady state is the platform doing its job: every agent on the record, independently measured against the standard your company wrote, with findings current as of the last action and evidence ready when an auditor, an insurer, a regulator, or a customer asks. The standard evolves with the business; the measurement and the record keep pace.

How they fit together

Three engagements, one direction.

Each engagement makes the next one cheaper and faster. The alignment session gives your team the language the budget conversation needs. The budget sprint funds the governance work as a planned 2027 line and spares you the mid-year fight for it. The diagnostic produces the specification that governs deployment and measurement.

Run in sequence, they take an organization from "we should probably talk about this" to agents on the record inside two quarters. Started anywhere, each one is complete on its own terms.

Why specification is where this ends up

Governance without a specification is just surveillance.

Whatever rung you start on, the work converges on the same object: a written, signed standard for how your agents behave. Measuring an agent against a standard nobody wrote down produces opinions. A finding requires a written standard the agent can meet or fail. Every measurement, every audit answer, and every piece of evidence downstream inherits its authority from that document.

This is why the consulting practice and the platform run as a single practice. The engagements produce the standard; the platform proves conduct against it, continuously, from a party with nothing to gain.

Who delivers it

Direct, or through a certified partner.

Applied Identities owns its methods and delivers them directly. Certified consulting partners deliver the same methods under license for larger or multi-region engagements. Either way, the standard your agents are measured against is produced under the method owner's direct authority, and the measurement that follows is performed by a party independent of whoever built or runs your agents.
