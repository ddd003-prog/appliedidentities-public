03What every agent gets

Every agent is a new employee. None of them were onboarded.

An agent in production sends the email, quotes the price, and answers the customer under your name. A person doing that work gets an identity, a defined role, boundaries, and a review. Agents just get deployed. Governance starts by issuing each agent what a person gets on day one.

A job description

What is this agent for, what may it decide, and where does its authority end? Written down, and signed.

Defined authority

Who gave it permission, and for what? Authority is granted narrowly, by name, and can be withdrawn on demand.

Limits that hold

Ceilings, scopes, and escalation rules that are enforced while the agent works, not reviewed after.

A public record

A live file anyone can check: who the agent is, who authorized it, and its current status. Never cached, never taken on faith.

04What you get

Findings you can act on.

The record rolls up into Behavioral Findings: five measured dimensions and a grade band, current as of the last action rather than the last quarter. Findings are built to be used, not filed. They tell your team and your vendor what to change, and then they prove whether the change worked. We never tune your agent; independence depends on that. We hand you the signal, and we measure the result.

MeasureFind the patternChange the agent or the standardMeasure againProve it worked

Find the pattern, not just the score

Every finding traces back to the interactions that produced it, down to the action. A score says something is off. The trace says what, and where.

See drift before it is a problem

An agent that met the standard in week one and drifts by week nine shows up as a trend against its own history, not as an incident.

Know how it holds under pressure

People push agents: urgency, authority, incentives, social proof. The model measures whether behavior holds when it is pushed, not only when it is easy.

Compare like with like

One standard, applied the same way to every agent, every vendor, every version. Before and after a change. One agent against another. Build against buy.

Change the standard on purpose

When the business changes, the standard changes with it, as a new version. Every finding states which version it was scored against, so the history stays readable.

Prove the fix worked

After the change, the same measurement runs again. The grade moves or it does not, and either way it is on the record.

The loop, on one agent

The company reads the findings, sees the pattern in the traces, rewrites one rule and narrows one tool scope. The same measurement runs again. Two dimensions move, three hold, and the change is on the record against the same standard. That is the whole point of measuring: not a grade, a lever.

Scoring today covers, on every action, whether the agent stayed within its authority and behaved the way its standard says. Consistency over time, behavior under pressure, and values expressed are defined in the model and activate by rubric release.

05Evidence

Meet a governed agent.

A standard she cannot edit

Authority: almost none, by design

Her tool allowlist is nearly empty. She holds no wallet authority and no payment capability. Non-commercial, on purpose.

Every conversation scored
