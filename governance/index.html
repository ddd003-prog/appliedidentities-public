Behavioral Governance

Independent attestation, action by action.

You write the Behavioral Manifest, the standard your agent works to. We measure every action against it and sign what we saw.

Talk to us See a record verified

The argument

An organization vouching for its own agent's behavior is self-attestation, the same structural problem as a company grading its own compliance. The measured party cannot keep the score. Credible governance needs an observer with nothing to protect: independent of the company deploying the agent, and of the vendor that built it.

How it works

Two planes.

The governance plane

In the cloud, holding what you have decided: the agent's Behavioral Manifest and its authorization.

The observation plane

Scribe runs on the machine where the agent works. Each action becomes an independent attestation, scored against the Manifest and signed. Attestations group into traces by interaction, and each trace is hashed, so the sequence of what happened is fixed and cannot be quietly edited. Traces accumulate in the agent's Action Log.

Behavioral Findings

For the people accountable for an agent, the record rolls up into Behavioral Findings: five measured dimensions and a grade band, current as of the last action rather than the last quarter. You see current findings on every agent you run.

Built to answer

When an auditor, an insurer, or a regulator asks how an agent has behaved, the layer is built to answer with a signed judgment over a trace, without opening your logs. The judgment stands on its own; the underlying record stays yours.
