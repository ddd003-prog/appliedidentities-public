The argument

An organization vouching for its own agent's behavior is self-attestation. No company signs off on its own books; the measured party cannot keep the score. Credible governance needs an observer with nothing at stake in the outcome: independent of the company deploying the agent, and of the vendor that built it.

who-keeps-the-score
self-attestation: measured party keeps the score rejected
company vouches for its own agent
vendor vouches for the agent it built
independent observer: nothing at stake accepted

How it works

Two planes.

The governance plane · in the cloud

In the cloud, holding what you have decided: the agent's Behavioral Manifest and its authorization.

The observation plane · on the machine

Scribe runs on the machine where the agent works. Each action becomes an independent attestation, scored against the Manifest and signed. Attestations group into traces by interaction, and each trace is hashed, so the sequence of what happened is fixed and cannot be quietly edited. Traces accumulate in the agent's Action Log.
