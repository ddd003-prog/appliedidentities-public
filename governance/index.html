Behavioral Governance

Independent attestation, action by action.

You set the specification and the authority. We watch the agent work and attest, one action at a time, whether behavior matched.

Talk to us Verify a record

The argument

An organization vouching for its own agent's behavior is self-attestation, the same structural problem as a company grading its own compliance. The measured party cannot keep the score. Credible governance needs an observer with nothing to protect, which is the layer we build: independent of the company deploying the agent and of the vendor that built it.

How it works

Two planes.

The governance plane

In the cloud, holding what you've decided: the agent's specification and its authorization.

The observation plane

On the machine where the agent works. For each action, an independent attestation is recorded: performed or not performed, against the specification. Attestations compose into traces, and each trace is hashed, so the sequence of what happened is fixed and cannot be quietly edited.

The record

Traces accumulate in the agent's action log, and compliance is computed over it as a standing view, current as of the last action rather than the last quarter. When an auditor, a partner, or your own board asks how the agent has behaved, the answer is a record, on file, attested by a party that isn't you.
