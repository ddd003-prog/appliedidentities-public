Every company now runs the same operational loop: agents, orchestration, workflows, and the models underneath, rented from the same vendors at the same speed. Capability stopped being an advantage when everyone could buy it.

What stays scarce sits above the loop: the standard your company writes, measurement someone independent performs, and a record that survives scrutiny. That layer is the work below.

An agent that represents your company deserves the treatment you give a hire. Papers on day one. A review conducted by someone who doesn't report to you. And only then: customers, and the company card.

That lifecycle is the platform.

Agent Identity

Identity your company stands behind.

Before an agent works for your company, it needs what an employee gets on day one: a defined role, an understanding of how your company operates, and authority in writing. The platform issues each agent that identity as signed documents covering who it is, how it works, and what it is permitted to do, with permissions that can be read and revoked at any time. Each identity is anchored in a public registry.

Behavioral Governance

Your agents, independently measured.

You set the standard for how your agents behave. An independent observer measures every action against it as it happens, signs the result, and keeps it in a record neither you nor your vendor can edit. Grading your own agents is self-attestation; the measured party cannot keep the score. The record rolls up into current findings on every agent.

Governed Commerce

An agent you'd let talk to customers.

A brand ambassador agent answers customers, recommends products, and closes the sale: work most companies won't hand to an agent yet, because they can't govern one. That is the work this governance exists for: written authority, independent measurement, and a public record when money moves. Scoped permissions, price ceilings, and immediate revocation are live today.

Embodied Oversight

Oversight for agents with bodies.

When agents drive robots, the same chain of authority applies and the measurement moves to the physical world: behavior observed live, divergence measured as it happens, evidence signed. A public demo runs the complete oversight pipeline against a simulated fleet. The robots are simulation scenes; the measurement is live computation.
