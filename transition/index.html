Rail 01 · Payment

How agents move money.

Stablecoin micropayments settle machine-to-machine in seconds. Card networks are adapting in parallel. The plumbing works today.

Payment rails answer one question: can this agent pay?
They answer nothing else.

Sources: Coinbase · Google Cloud · PayPal
Rail 02 · Intent

How agents prove a human said yes.

Cryptographically signed mandates travel with the transaction: a verifiable record that a person authorized this purchase, at this price, from this merchant.

Intent rails answer: did someone authorize this transaction?
One answer, per transaction. Nothing more.

Sources: AP2 specification · W3C Verifiable Credentials
Rail 03 · Governance

The rail nobody built.

Payment rails verify the money. Intent rails verify the transaction. Neither verifies the agent: who stands behind it, what authority it holds, and whether it behaves as specified on transaction ten thousand.

A mandate proves a purchase was authorized.
Nothing proves the agent itself is governed.

Named by regulators: EU AI Act · NIST AI RMF · ISO/IEC 42001
