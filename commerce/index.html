What your company governs

Price ceiling

The most the agent may spend or accept on a single transaction. Above it, the sale stops and escalates.

Product scope

Which catalog, which lines, which customers. Outside the scope, the agent does not act.

Discount and payment authority

Whether the agent may discount, and whether it may move money at all, and how much.

Escalation rules

What hands off to a person, and when. The declined sale below is an escalation, not an error.

Revocation

Authority withdrawn on demand. Every verification path rejects the credential afterward.
